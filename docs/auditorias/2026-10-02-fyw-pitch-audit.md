## 1. Find Your Way Hackathon — context (esp. Costa Rica)

### 1.1 What it is

**Find Your Way** is a Stellar builder hackathon run with Tellus Cooperative / Stellar Passport, designed so teams turn ideas into functional Stellar projects, practice pitching, and prepare for **HackMeridian** (the official Meridian conference hackathon).

There are at least two related Luma surfaces:

## 3. Reverse-engineering patterns — what winners share

### 3.1 Product patterns

1. **Demoable payment / money flow in &lt;60 seconds**  
   Escrow fund → milestone approve → release (Lance, Payroll Genius, Trustless Work–shaped apps); or one-click swap/action (Swifli); or deposit→yield (DeFindex, 4Bridge principal-preserving donate).

2. **Obvious Stellar “why”**  
   Fast/cheap settlement, USDC/stable rails, Passkeys/smart wallet UX, Soroban escrow/lending, Telegram or link distribution — not “could be any L2.”

3. **Composability with named ecosystem primitives**  
   Winners name **Blend, Soroswap, DeFindex, Reflector, MoneyGram, Passkeys**. Judges reward “built *on* the Blueprint,” not only custom silos.

4. **Real-world problem framing**  
   Payroll, freelancers, donations without draining principal, receivables/invoice finance, remittances, royalties — SME/emerging-market pain &gt; speculative meme.

5. **UX that non-crypto users can follow**  
   Strooper (Telegram), Swifli (links), Decaf (cash-out), DeFindex (savings metaphor). Seed phrases and explorer-only demos lose.

6. **Working prototype &gt; slideware**  
   DoraHacks Blend and Meridian culture: repo + live demo. Incomplete but runnable beats polished Figma.

### 3.2 Pitch / video patterns

1. **Problem → Solution → Live demo → Stellar integration → Ask** in that order (matches FYW’s stated video content).  
2. **Show the happy path on camera** (wallet create → fund → milestone → payout). Narrate while clicking.  
3. **English** for global / Passport / Meridian path; keep jargon light.  
4. **One memorable mechanic** (“lossless donate,” “blink link,” “salary vault earns yield,” “freelancer escrow + dispute”).  
5. **Team slide + repo + deployed link** (EasyA submission culture).  
6. Length discipline: Blend asks ≤5 min; for FYW, **aim 60–90s core demo reel** (+ longer cut if Passport allows).

### 3.3 Judging culture (inferred)

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Completion & innovation weighted high | Blend DoraHacks rubric |
| Impact / access / interoperability | i³ categories; Cash-to-DeFi track |
| Ship with ecosystem partners | Composability track at HackMeridian 2025 |
| Presentation still matters | Finalists pitch on Meridian stage; EasyA Shark Tank |

## 5. Pitch foundation

### 5.1 Spoken pitch outline (30–60 seconds)

**0–10s — Hook (problem)**  
“Event organizers and NGOs still pay volunteers with spreadsheets and WhatsApp receipts—money moves late, and nobody can prove a milestone was done.”

**10–25s — Solution**  
“Hyto puts volunteer and event payouts into **milestone escrow on Stellar**. Organizers fund a Trustless Work escrow; helpers get a **Cavos wallet** in seconds; when a milestone is done, **Mile**—our AI reviewer—checks the evidence before funds release.”

**25–45s — Proof (demo narration)**  
“Watch: I create an event, lock USDC on testnet, a volunteer submits photos for ‘setup complete,’ Mile flags pass, I approve—and the payout hits their wallet. Every step is on Stellar.”

**45–60s — Why Stellar + ask**  
“We chose Stellar for fast, cheap settlement and programmable escrow—not another payment spreadsheet. We’re shipping for ZEEK and events in Costa Rica, and Find Your Way is our runway to HackMeridian. We’re Hyto—help us pay the people who show up.”

### 5.2 Pitch-video shot list / script beats (60–90 seconds)

| Time | Shot | Audio / on-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8s | B-roll: chaotic volunteer chat / spreadsheet (or stilts) → cut to Hyto logo | “Paying volunteers shouldn’t feel like detective work.” |
| 8–18s | Face-to-camera or clean title cards | Problem in one sentence; “Hyto” + “Stellar” badges |
| 18–28s | Screen: create event + fund TW escrow (show amount + roles) | “Organizer funds a Trustless Work v2 escrow on Stellar testnet.” |
| 28–40s | Screen: Cavos wallet onboarding for volunteer | “Helpers get a Cavos wallet—no seed phrase theater.” |
| 40–55s | Screen: milestone submit + **Mile** AI review UI | “Mile reviews evidence before anyone releases funds.” |
| 55–70s | Screen: approve → release → wallet balance / tx | “Milestone paid. Transparent. On Stellar.” |
| 70–80s | Architecture one-liner (diagram): Event → Escrow → Mile → Cavos | “Composable escrow + embedded wallets + AI review.” |
| 80–90s | Team + ZEEK/CR + CTA | “Hyto · Find Your Way Costa Rica · next: HackMeridian Lisbon. Repo + demo in description.” |

**Production tips from winner culture:** show real clicks; avoid slides-only; keep UI English; end on a live success state, not a roadmap.

## 6. Gaps / risks for Hyto vs winners

| Risk | Why it hurts | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Testnet-only** | Meridian/i³ winners often show mainnet volume or production partners | Be explicit; show realistic testnet volume UX; roadmap mainnet + TW audit |
| **TW v2 beta / unaudited** | Judges may fear escrow risk | One-slide honesty + Lab/Viewer proof; don’t overclaim production readiness |
| **Category crowding** | Lance / Payroll Genius already own “escrow + work pay” | Niche: **volunteers/events + Mile AI evidence**; ZEEK distribution |
| **Weak “Stellar why”** | Generic escrow could be any chain | Stress Stellar payment rails + Cavos + TW on Soroban; show tx latency/fees |
| **AI theater** | Mile looks bolted-on if always “approve” | Demo false positive / needs-revision path |
| **Incomplete Passport submission** | Luma RSVP ≠ entry | Checklist §4.3 |
| **University Track confusion** | Chile-only | File under General Track |
| **Spanish vs English** | Local meetup Spanish; Meridian English | English video + English app; optional Spanish subtitle |
| **Demo fragility** | Winners live-demo under stress | Record backup screencast; pre-fund wallets; scripted accounts |
| **Missing public metrics** | Decaf/Etherfuse win with numbers | Even testnet: #escrows, #milestones released, time-to-wallet |
| **Incomplete competitor intel** | Star Lends / full EasyA UX podium unknown | Don’t overfit; stick to verified patterns |
| **Deadline risk** | CR close **5 Oct 16:00** — days away as of 2 Oct | Freeze features; prioritize video + happy path |
